# MonoGit v0.3.4 Release Notes

**MonoGit v0.3.4** introduces 1-line mouse wheel precision scrolling, automatic panel state cleanup when navigating back to Panel 1, and a complete visual redesign of the Shortcuts modal (`?` / `ctrl+p`) with full keybinding coverage.

---

## ⌨️ Clean Multi-Column Shortcuts Modal (`?` / `ctrl+p`)
- **Flat Multi-Column Layout**: Eliminated nested bordered cards inside the scrollable viewport. The modal now features a single clean rounded border with seamless vertical column dividers (`" │ "`) that scroll smoothly without detached borders or double vertical pipes (`│ │`).
- **Modern Hierarchy**: Section headings are styled in bold cyan (`ui.ColorCyan`), underscored with subtle horizontal dividers, and paired with bold accent-colored key badges.
- **Complete Keybinding Coverage**: All 47 keybindings from `keys.go` are now documented and organized across 10 thematic sections (`CURSOR & SCROLL`, `PANELS & FOCUS`, `SELECTION & SYSTEM`, `WORKSPACE & TOOLS`, `REMOTE SYNC`, `SEARCH & TAGS`, `COMMIT WIZARD`, `BRANCHES & REBASE`, `DIFFS & CONFLICTS`, `STASH MODE & LOGS`).
- **Even Balancing**: Shortcuts are evenly distributed across columns (27, 24, and 23 lines in 3-column mode), fitting completely on standard terminal heights without unnecessary scrolling.

---

## 🖱️ 1-Line Mouse Scroll Precision
- **Panel 1 Precision**: Mouse wheel scrolling over the repository list now advances exactly 1 item per notch instead of skipping 2 items.
- **Debounce & Release Filter**: Added a 45ms rate-limiting debounce on wheel events and filtered out `MouseActionRelease` packets from terminal mouse reporting, ensuring responsive and reliable scrolling across viewports.

---

## 🧭 Panel Navigation Auto-Reset
- **Automatic Panel State Cancellation**: When navigating back from Panel 2 to Panel 1 using `h`, `←` (arrow left), `1`, or mouse click/scroll, special modes (`showBranches`, `showStashes`, `showConflicts`) are automatically closed.
- **Immediate Overview Restoration**: Panel 2 immediately returns to the default repository overview and activity log, restoring full access to Panel 1 commands without requiring manual dismissal.
